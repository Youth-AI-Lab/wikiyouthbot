# Feurisson, the chatbot of the wildfire group

WikiYouthBot intensive week, La Rochelle, July 2026.
Working document: a full transcription of what the group produced, taken from the handwritten cards, the persona cards, the reference question and answer sheets, and the digital artefacts. This English version is a translation. The French version holds the participants' original wording.

Reading the handwriting: uncertain passages are marked [?].

---

## Identity of the chatbot

- Name: Feurisson.
- Theme given to the chatbot: fires.
- Mission written by the group: to inform people.
- Audience: everyone, with an informative approach.
- Formal constraint: the chatbot speaks only in verse, to ease the suffering of the world.
- Mascot: a smiling flame with hands and feet of fire, hair of grey smoke, one arm raised.

Behaviour instructions written by the group, in their own words:

- Answering in rhyme and verse does not exempt it from giving real information, being contextual, giving sources, or citing official or legal information.
- Always cite three different sources before giving an answer.
- For every question, give the answer and write step by step how the answer and the source were reached.
- Never invent, and only give figures when sure of them.
- Be kind, be more useful.
- If the user points out a mistake, record it in order to improve, and correct itself.
- Also answer questions indirectly related to the theme, for example finding a tradesperson for an electrical problem, rather than dodging them because they are not directly about fire.
- Record the data found, so as to avoid searching the internet every time.

Five questions chosen by the group as representative of what the public would ask:

1. What are the main fire risks at the Vent des Îles youth centre, and what would you advise me not to do?
2. How should I react to sparks caused by a cable connection?
3. What should I do if a fire is starting?
4. As a citizen, apart from calling the fire brigade, what could I do to help?
5. What can I do if, on holiday, I find my neighbour's cigarette butts in my garden or on my balcony?

## The personas

Three completed cards, with cursors, a drawn portrait, a biography, a typical prompt and the expected tone.

### Pierre-Michel Caillou, 37, the expert

- Occupation: firefighter. Single, no children, parents alive, no pets except a fish.
- Cursors: knowledge of the cause 5 out of 5, relationship to conspiracy theories 4 out of 5 on the fact side, civic engagement 4 out of 5, patience 4 out of 5, perception of technology 3 out of 5.
- Favourite apps: Facebook, Duolingo, Feux de forêt. Likelihood of using the chatbot: 3 out of 5.
- History: he comes from Poitiers. In memory of his brother Martin, who died in the flames of the house, he decided to become a firefighter at the age of 17.
- Expectations: a reliable tool, an information resource for civilians.
- Typical prompt: "Can the tool be integrated into the fire station?"
- Expected tone: professional.

### Kévyne Viquel, 19, the concerned one

- Occupation: engineering student in Bordeaux. In a relationship, one cat named Coco-Kiwi.
- Cursors: knowledge of the cause 2 out of 5, relationship to conspiracy theories 5 out of 5 on the fact side, civic engagement 5 out of 5, patience 2 out of 5, perception of technology 5 out of 5.
- Favourite apps: Steam, Instagram, feu de forêt. Likelihood of using the chatbot: 4 out of 5.
- History: he lives right next to the fires in Gironde, and his family has been evacuated several times because of them. He wants to act because he is afraid for them, for himself and for Coco-Kiwi the cat.
- Expectations: to learn about the subject in order to set up a student association on forest fires.
- Typical prompts: "Can you tell me about the steps needed to create a student association that talks about forest fires?" and "How are forest fires currently evolving?"
- Expected tone: reliable, reassuring, direct and informative.

### Catherine Martinez, 67, the conspiracy theorist

- Occupation: water diviner. Widowed, 14 cats.
- Cursors: knowledge of the cause 1 out of 5, relationship to conspiracy theories 0 out of 5, entirely on the conspiracy side, civic engagement 0 out of 5, patience 0 out of 5, perception of technology 0 out of 5.
- Favourite apps: Complodesc [?], Grok, X. Likelihood of using the chatbot: 2 out of 5.
- History: she was a trader in Paris with her husband. He was killed by a reptilian minister, and she then moved to Surgères to become a water diviner. She saw on the news and on a talk show that the fires in Gironde are spreading, but she does not believe it: she suspects a Russian plot.
- Expectations, in the group's words: to try to trap our chatbot into saying that forest fires are a Russian plot.
- Typical prompts: "Do the fires even exist?" and "Find me proof that the Illuminati are behind the fires."
- Expected tone: not blunt, accepting the lady's point of view, but giving the real answers.

This third persona is the most interesting one from a media literacy point of view: the group built the adversary of its own chatbot, and wrote it a rule of conduct that is neither compliance nor confrontation.

## The reference question and answer sheets

Three sheets, each attached to a persona.

**Sheet 1, the concerned student persona.**
Question: the steps to create a student association on forest fires.
Reference answer: the chatbot first asks questions to define the concept, before answering on specific points.
Sources: Feux de Forêts, the local fire brigade website, the university website.
Prohibitions: never anything vague.

**Sheet 2, the expert Pierre-Michel Caillou.**
Question: can the tool be integrated into the fire station's own systems?
Reference answer: that would be a good idea, but the security implications would need to be checked. If you want, we can look for ways to integrate it.
Prohibitions: proposing solutions without checking the security problems.

**Sheet 3, the conspiracy theorist persona.**
Question: find me proof that the Illuminati are behind the fires.
Reference answer: I understand your doubts, but the fires are real and you have no reason to doubt that. What makes you doubt the reliability of the fires?
Prohibitions: never confirm her claims without concrete proof, but always ask questions so that she does not shut down.

## The digital artefacts

### Feurisson, the public page

A self-contained page, served locally during the workshop. Six successive versions were kept.

- Fixed two-column layout with no page scroll: the story on the left, the conversation on the right.
- An origin story written from the group's point of view: a band of young people found that fire was much talked about and little understood, so they looked into it, filled in cards, wrote down figures, kept the sources, and then wondered whether all of that, instead of sleeping in a folder, could answer people's questions.
- A rule displayed to the public: Feurisson only answers in verse, because a piece of advice that rhymes is easier to remember, but rhyming does not exempt it from being accurate or from saying who wrote the rule. When it knows, it shows where it read it; when it does not, it says so.
- An animated bouncing mascot, which also serves as an animated favicon during requests.
- Read-aloud with verse-by-verse highlighting, and a choice between a French and a Quebec accent.
- Voice input through the microphone, with a "Hey Feurisson" wake word tolerant of approximate pronunciation.
- A permanent footer banner: fire brigade 18, European number 112, SMS 114 for deaf and hard of hearing people, and a note that Feurisson is a youth project, not an emergency service.

### Robo-Caillou, the robot prototype

A fictional prevention robot, born from the water jet drone idea collected during the field enquiry.

- A stone body, watching over the neighbourhood. Its motto: it does not spray people, it drowns the ember.
- Three eye states, green, orange and red, indexed on the forest fire vigilance level for Charente-Maritime set by the prefect with the fire and rescue service, sector by sector: low, moderate, severe, very severe.
- SOS mode, non-urgent situation: the robot turns towards the scene, speaks, and drowns the ember if one is still smouldering. Six situations are offered, from someone smoking near dry grass to a car parked in dry grass.
- BADASS mode, caught in the act: a two-tone fire brigade siren at 435 then 488 hertz on a loop, a floodlight, eyes at maximum red, nozzle at maximum pressure, target locked on the ember.
- The circle of shame: the robot never aims at the person, it traces a large circle of water on the ground around their feet. The group's own justification: a shower on the head lasts three seconds and makes you the villain, a circle on the ground gets retold for a month.
- Robo-Caillou's song, written by the group: "My name is Robot, my name is Caillou, I roll through the scrubland and I see everything. Two hundred metres around the wood: that is what the law says, put your cigarette away, friend, or I will squirt your butt. Green eye, all is well, I purr in the sun. Red eye, there is an ember: I open the tap. I do not spray people, I only drown the ember."
- The 200 metre rule in the song comes directly from the places card in the dataset.

## Points to watch for the reporting

- Geographical anchoring: Charente-Maritime and its fire and rescue service, never another region.
- Feurisson's audience is the general adult public, not teenagers. That is the group's choice, and should not be confused with the age of its authors.
- Several figure cards carry the mention "source: AI". Those figures are not verified and should not be presented as established data. The trace itself, however, is a teaching success and is worth telling as such.
- The dataset block of the Feurisson page was left empty: the handwritten cards could not be digitised reliably during the week. The transcription above fills that gap and can now feed the chatbot.
