Youth Ai Lab · WikiYouthBot

Chatbots built by young people, and the kit that made them

WikiYouthBot is an intensive week in which small groups pick a societal cause, investigate it in their own neighbourhood, and turn what they gather into a chatbot that speaks about it.

The chatbot starts out as a confident fabricator. It only becomes reliable to the extent the group writes it so: rules, sources, local material, personas. This site holds three things: what the participants produced, the documentation of how each chatbot was configured, and the full facilitation kit, ready to be reused by another lab.

Productions · Lab Les Pacman's, La Rochelle

27 to 31 July 2026. Eleven participants aged 11 to 16, in two groups. Published as the groups left them at the end of the week.

Wildfire prevention

Feurisson

A chatbot that answers only in verse, because a piece of advice that rhymes is easier to remember. Rhyming does not exempt it from being accurate, or from saying where it read something.

Needs your own Anthropic API key to answer. Open Feurisson → School bullying

Superbot

A chatbot that answers on school bullying from reference answers with their sources displayed, and that drops everything else the moment someone says they are not doing well.

Answers from the group's own base, no key needed. Open Superbot → Robot prototype

Robo-Caillou

The neighbourhood watcher. Its eyes follow the wildfire vigilance level, and it never sprays a person: it traces a circle of water on the ground around them and drowns the ember.

Fully interactive, no key needed. Open Robo-Caillou →

Facilitation kit

The tools built for the week, published so that another lab can run the same sequence. Everything runs in a browser. Only the press conference needs an API key, and a free one is enough.

Tool

Mission of the week

The sealed envelope and the roadmap used to open each morning: the mission of the week, the teams, and the sixteen steps that take a generic AI to a specialised chatbot.

Open the mission → Tool

AI press conference

The central device that lets a whole group question an AI out loud, as journalists at a press conference. Choose the city, the topic, the register, and whether the AI speaks with the browser voice or a natural one.

Needs a free Gemini key, or a Claude key. Runs best from a local server, for the microphone. Open the press conference → Printable
